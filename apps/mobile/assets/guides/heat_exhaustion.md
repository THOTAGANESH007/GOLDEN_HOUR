# Heat Exhaustion & Heatstroke Response

> **EMERGENCY NOTICE:** Heatstroke is a life-threatening emergency. If a person displays confusion, slurred speech, loss of consciousness, or hot dry skin, dial emergency services (112 / 911 / 108) immediately and begin aggressive body cooling.

---

### Distinguishing Heat Exhaustion vs Heatstroke

| Feature | Heat Exhaustion | Heatstroke (Life-Threatening) |
|---|---|---|
| **Body Temperature** | Usually elevated (< 40°C / 104°F) | Extremely high (≥ 40°C / 104°F) |
| **Mental State** | Intact, dizziness, fatigue | Confusion, agitation, delirium, coma |
| **Skin Condition** | Pale, cool, clammy with heavy sweating | Hot, flushed, red (often dry, no sweat) |
| **Pulse Rate** | Rapid and weak | Rapid and strong/bounding |
| **Urgency** | Urgent cooling, hydration | Critical Emergency (Call 112 now) |

---

### Step-by-Step Response Protocol

#### 1. Move to a Cool, Shaded Environment
- Immediately bring the person indoors into an air-conditioned room, or into shaded, well-ventilated shelter.
- Lay the person down flat and elevate their legs slightly (about 15-30 cm) to assist blood circulation.

#### 2. Loosen & Remove Excess Clothing
- Remove tight jackets, hats, shoes, socks, and heavy outer garments to expose skin to air flow.

#### 3. Initiate Active Cooling
- **Mist & Fan:** Spray or sponge the person with cool tap water while continuously fanning them with a handheld fan or electric fan.
- **Ice Packs on Vascular Points:** Apply cold wet towels or wrapped ice packs to areas where large blood vessels pass close to the surface:
  - Both sides of the neck
  - Armpits
  - Groin
- **Immersion Cooling:** If heatstroke is confirmed and an ice bath or cool tub is available, submerge the person up to the neck while keeping head supported.

#### 4. Safe Hydration Guidelines
- **If conscious and alert:** Offer small sips of cool water, oral rehydration salts (ORS), or electrolyte sports drinks.
- ❌ **If confused, lethargic, or unconscious: DO NOT give any fluids by mouth**, as this risks choking and airway obstruction.

---

### Warning Signs Requiring Immediate Emergency Transport
- The person does not improve within 30 minutes of cooling.
- Vomiting repeatedly and unable to keep fluids down.
- Onset of seizure activity, hallucinations, or unresponsiveness.
