# Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (Hands-Only CPR)

> **EMERGENCY NOTICE:** Call emergency services (112 / 911 / 108) immediately or instruct someone nearby to call before beginning CPR. If an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) is available, send someone to retrieve it immediately.

---

### When to Perform Hands-Only CPR
Perform CPR when an individual is:
- **Unresponsive:** Does not react to shouting or gentle shoulder taps.
- **Not Breathing Normally:** No breathing, or only occasional gasping/agonal breaths.

---

### Step-by-Step Response Protocol

#### 1. Verify Scene Safety & Responsiveness
- Ensure the surrounding area is safe for you and the person.
- Tap the person's shoulder firmly and shout: *"Are you okay?"*
- Look at their chest for regular breathing movement (spend no more than 10 seconds checking).

#### 2. Call Emergency Services & Request an AED
- Shout for nearby help. Direct a specific person: *"You, call 112 and look for an AED."*
- If alone with a mobile phone, place the call on speaker while beginning compressions.

#### 3. Position the Person
- Place the person flat on their back on a firm, flat surface (floor or ground).
- Kneel beside their chest.

#### 4. Hand Placement for Compressions
- Place the heel of one hand in the center of the chest (between the nipples / lower half of breastbone).
- Place the heel of your other hand directly on top of the first hand, interlocking your fingers.
- Keep your elbows locked and shoulders positioned directly over your hands.

#### 5. Deliver Chest Compressions
- **Depth:** Push hard, pressing down at least 5 cm (2 inches) deep.
- **Rate:** Push fast, at a cadence of 100 to 120 compressions per minute (to the beat of *"Stayin' Alive"*).
- **Recoil:** Allow the chest to return completely to its normal position between each compression. Do not lean continuously on the chest.

#### 6. Continue Compressions Until:
- Obvious signs of recovery or spontaneous movement appear.
- Emergency services or trained responders arrive and take over.
- An AED arrives and is ready to analyze the rhythm.
- You are physically exhausted and another bystander can alternate compressions.

---

### Using an Automated External Defibrillator (AED)
1. Turn on the AED and follow its spoken voice prompts immediately.
2. Bare the person's chest and wipe dry if wet.
3. Attach the adhesive pads as shown on the pad diagrams (upper right chest, lower left side).
4. Stand clear when the AED says *"Analyzing rhythm"* or *"Delivering shock"*.
5. Resume compressions immediately following a shock or if advised by the AED.

---

### Important Safety Reminders
- **Do not interrupt compressions** for more than 10 seconds.
- **Do not hesitate:** Hands-only CPR performed by bystanders significantly doubles or triples survival odds.
